    Where these numbers come from

    • How people use AI for each task: Anthropic Economic Index, used for the "Steady" speed.
    • What AI could do in each job: ILO generative AI exposure index (2025 refined version), used for the "Fast" speed.
    • Your tasks are matched to the national skills standard (NOSS) and then to international task lists (O*NET) so the data can be joined.
    • This prototype uses sample numbers shaped like these datasets. They are not real statistics.
