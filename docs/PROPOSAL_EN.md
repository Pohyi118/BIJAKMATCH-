# ReRole Green Proposal (English translation)

Translated from the Chinese proposal "ReRole Green 提案", dated Oct 21, 2026. This covers the summary and section 1 only. ReRole Green is the earlier name of the product now called BijakMatch.

## Summary

ReRole Green is a skills-investment navigator for Malaysian TVET workers. It reads the worker's certificates and real tasks, then tells them which abilities carry over to the green economy, how long the window of opportunity is, and what to do as a first step this week.

We do not predict "will you be replaced". We compare three paths, Stay, Evolve and Pivot, and every path has a source, a date and an explainable score.

The user ends up with a **Career Transition Card**: the chosen path, a first step that can be done within 4 weeks, cost and time, uncertainty, exit conditions, and a concrete contribution to SDG 4 and SDG 8.

## 1. The problem

Malaysia has hundreds of thousands of workers who need to transition, but TVET workers lack a tool that translates their real abilities into a feasible next step in the green economy.

TalentCorp's impact study estimates that about 620,000 jobs in 10 key sectors face convergence or displacement risk, and about 20% of these are linked to the green economy (The Edge). Phase 2 widens the scope to 22 sectors and identifies 22 emerging green roles (TalentCorp briefing).

### Our focus

| | |
|---|---|
| **User** | Manufacturing / E&E production-line technician, TVET background, employed, limited time and budget |
| **Moment of need** | Line automation is speeding up. They hear "AI will replace technicians" and do not know what to learn |
| **Target area** | Rooftop solar PV and EV charging. One slice, done in depth, not wide |
| **Core difference** | Three-track profile (certificates + workflow interview + evidence), NOSS task dictionary, regulatory gates, data-driven scenarios, Window Score, and a traceable personalised plan |

### Where TVET workers get stuck

1. **A CV cannot express their ability.** "Technician, 5 years" does not say which faults they can handle, which certificates they hold, or what evidence they have.
2. **AI impact is told as "job replacement".** What they need to know is which tasks are turning red and which are turning green, not a scare line.
3. **Green opportunity is told as "go learn solar".** No one says whether the skill is a long-term asset or a passing trend, whether their learning speed can keep up with the window, or which statutory licence they still lack.

### Core user question

As a working TVET worker: which of my current tasks carry over? Is competition for the target job already saturated? With my weekly time and budget, what is my first step?
